AI Healthcare Architecture

CureConnect — Deep Dive

AI-Driven Early Risk Evaluation & Medical Triage Guidance

Problem Statement & Motivation

When acute symptoms arise, patients often lack clear guidance on whether to rush to an emergency room, visit an outpatient clinic, or practice home first-aid. CureConnect provides an accessible, non-diagnostic guidance layer to streamline this critical decision-making process.

Core Features & Architecture

  • Contextual Triage Logic: Weighted symptom risk scoring based on age, symptom duration, and severity indicators.
  • Urgency Classification: Three-tier priority dispatch (Emergency, General Consultation, Self-Monitoring).
  • Immediate First-Aid Protocols: Step-by-step guidance presented in accessible, non-technical language.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Zero personal identifiable data retention on initial evaluation screens.

Technology Stack

Python Rule & Risk Evaluation Engine JavaScript (ES6+) Semantic HTML5 & Modern CSS
Notice: CureConnect is intended solely for risk awareness and navigation assistance. It does not replace certified professional medical diagnosis.

Self-Watering Planter — Deep Dive

Smart Agro-Climatic Sub-Irrigation & Moisture Optimization

System Concept & Goal

Conventional surface watering loses up to 40% of water through surface evaporation and runoff. The Self-Watering Planter uses bottom-up sub-irrigation capillary action combined with microcontroller threshold gating to supply moisture exactly when root-zone moisture drops below calibrated thresholds.

Engineering Highlights

  • Sub-Surface Irrigation Delivery: Minimizes evaporation and prevents fungal root diseases caused by surface over-watering.
  • Automated Actuation: Microcontroller-based relay triggering based on analog soil moisture sensor readings.
  • Conservation-First Design: Drastically reduces manual intervention and optimizes water utilization.

Hardware & Software Stack

C++ Firmware Microcontroller Logic Soil Moisture Sensor Sub-Irrigation Basin
